Report

Physical

Compact, strong build for a middle infielder rather than the usual wiry shortstop frame. Quick-twitch athlete with explosive first movements. Limited projection left in the body; future gains come from strength and lift, not size.

Hitting

Short, direct stroke with quick hands and good feel for the barrel. Uses the whole field and handles velocity. Breaking balls and changeups are the test: chase and swing-and-miss rise against spin, and he needed a reset after a rough first look at High-A in 2025 (.517 OPS, 25 games). He answered it by hitting .298/.384/.420 in a return trip at 19. Walk rate dipped in a short Double-A sample, as expected for a teenager in the Southern League.

Power

More strength than the home run totals show. He has reached 113 mph exit velocity, but the swing is built for line drives and gaps (three homers, 16 doubles and triples in 2026). Game power will depend on getting the ball in the air more often; average power is a realistic outcome.

Speed / baserunning

The carrying tool. True 70 runner who uses it: 35 steals in 70 games this year, 118 in his career. Aggressive and disruptive. Takes extra bases and puts pressure on the defense.

Defense

At shortstop the actions and footwork run hot and cold, and range to both sides and decisions under pressure are below average today. Moved to third base at Double-A to play next to Jesús Made, where the arm and first step fit better. The highest-upside landing spot is center field: the speed should give him plus range once reads and routes develop. The bat plays at any of the three.

Makeup

Plays with energy and competes. Came back from an April hospitalization and more than three weeks away without losing production, and earned a mid-August promotion to Double-A in his age-19 season.

Health

Fractured left fibula on a stolen-base attempt of third base in early September, ending his season. The recovery timeline is the near-term question, and with his game built on speed it is worth tracking his running in spring.

Summary

Peña is one of the best prospects in the Brewers' system and one of the best in baseball: a 19-year-old with 70 speed, contact skill well ahead of his age and real strength in the swing, who already reached Double-A. The bat and legs make him an everyday player wherever he lands. Center field would turn his speed into defensive value and give him the highest ceiling, with third base a strong alternate. As he learns to handle spin and lift the ball, he projects as a top-of-the-order regular who impacts the game on both sides.
