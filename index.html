Projects

Work done for a team uses licensed data and is internal to that team, so it is described here without results.

2026 · HockeyInternal

Opponent-adjusted team ratings and pre-scout reports

Rates every team in a league on offense and defense after adjusting for the opponents it has faced, then produces a one-page report for each upcoming opponent: projected matchup, strengths, weak spots and a side-by-side profile.

RRidge regressionAutomated reporting

2026 · HockeyInternal

Which metrics can a staff trust?

A study of roughly 250 performance metrics that separates repeatable team traits from game-to-game noise, then checks which traits carry over to results in games they never saw.

RSplit-half reliabilityPermutation testing

2026 · HockeyInternal

What goes with winning: a game-level model

An elastic-net model across every league game that identifies which parts of play go with winning and which lead to a positive expected-goal differential, re-fitted repeatedly so only durable signals are reported.

RElastic netGrouped cross-validation

2026 · Basketball

NBA shot make probability model

A model that estimates the probability an NBA shot is made from player-tracking and shot-context data. A custom defensive-pressure metric, contester density, ranked third in importance, and jump-shot accuracy fell from about 42% under the lightest pressure to about 32% under the heaviest.

RXGBoostFeature engineering
